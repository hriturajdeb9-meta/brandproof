How Brandproof works

A pre-publication checkpoint: every draft is checked against the Brand Kit before anyone publishes it. Code does everything it can; AI is used only where judgment is needed, and code checks the AI's answers.

The check, step by step

  1. Read. Text is taken from the text box or a .docx file. Images are resized and their colours measured. AI reads the words inside the image and where they are.
  2. Code checks. Glossary terms, banned phrases, required disclaimers, all-caps and hype emoji, readability, link text, inclusive language, image colours against the palette (CIEDE2000 distance) and text contrast (WCAG formula).
  3. AI checks. The AI finds claims and says which ledger fact each one is about, and reviews tone against the tone rules and their examples. It must quote the draft exactly and answer in strict JSON.
  4. Verify. Code throws away any AI finding whose quote isn't really in the draft or whose rule doesn't exist. Code, not AI, decides claim verdicts by comparing numbers and expiry dates with the Claim Ledger. AI rewrites are shown only if they keep locked phrases, use only ledger numbers and make no prohibited promises.
  5. Explain. Every finding shows its rule, source, method (code or AI), confidence and a fix. Nothing changes until a person accepts it.
  6. Route. Drafts with claims, rejected serious findings, open major findings or AI uncertainty go to a reviewer. Everything else is cleared automatically, and that is logged too.

Data and privacy

  • Demo data only. Paisaleaf, its rules, facts and sample drafts are fictional. Please don't upload confidential material to this demo.
  • Minimum data to the AI. Only the draft being checked and the relevant rules and facts are sent, never the whole Brand Kit or other drafts.
  • Storage. Drafts, versions, decisions, comments and the audit log are stored in {{ 'PostgreSQL' if storage == 'postgres' else 'SQLite' }} on our server. Images are re-encoded (which strips camera metadata) before they are stored.
  • Roles. This demo has no sign-in so judges can try every role. Each action is still recorded against the role that did it.
  • Coming in Round 3: personal data (emails, phone numbers) and confidential terms removed before any AI call, and serious AI findings double-checked.
