How Brandproof works

A pre-publication checkpoint: every draft is checked against the Brand Kit before anyone publishes it. Code does everything it can; AI is used only where judgment is needed, and code checks the AI's answers.

The check, step by step

  1. Read. Text is taken from the text box, a Word file or a PDF (page by page). Images are resized and their colours measured. AI reads the words inside the image and where they are. Personal data and confidential terms are masked before anything goes to the AI.
  2. Code checks. Glossary terms, banned phrases, required disclaimers, all-caps and hype emoji, readability, link text, inclusive language, image colours against the palette (CIEDE2000 distance) and text contrast (WCAG formula).
  3. AI checks. The AI finds claims and says which ledger fact each one is about, and reviews tone against the tone rules and their examples. It must quote the draft exactly and answer in strict JSON.
  4. Verify. Code throws away any AI finding whose quote isn't really in the draft or whose rule doesn't exist. Code, not AI, decides claim verdicts by comparing numbers and expiry dates with the Claim Ledger. AI rewrites are shown only if they keep locked phrases, use only ledger numbers and make no prohibited promises.
  5. Explain. Every finding shows its rule, source, method (code or AI), confidence and a fix. Nothing changes until a person accepts it.
  6. Second opinion. Blocker and major findings that depended on the AI are re-judged by a different model. If it disagrees or is unsure, the finding is marked "needs human judgment" and the draft goes to a reviewer.
  7. Route. Drafts with claims, rejected serious findings, open major findings or AI uncertainty go to a reviewer. Everything else is cleared automatically, and that is logged too.

AI disclosure and model settings

Each AI job has its own list of models. The first one that answers wins; a busy, slow or missing model is skipped and rested for a minute, so the next call goes straight to the backup. Models are chosen with server settings (AI_ROUTE_CLAIMS, AI_ROUTE_TONE, …), so they can be changed and compared without touching code.

Data, privacy and access

  • Workspaces. Each brand's rules, drafts, team and audit log live in its own workspace. {{ rls_note }}
  • Roles. Creators write and fix drafts; reviewers approve; brand managers own the Brand Kit and the team. The audit log is append-only: nobody can edit or delete it.
  • Sign-in. {{ auth_mode | capitalize }}. Sessions are signed cookies (HttpOnly, SameSite). The public demo workspace needs no account.
  • Masking before AI. Emails, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN and card numbers, and the workspace's confidential terms are replaced with placeholders like [EMAIL-1] before any text goes to an AI provider, and put back afterwards. Detection is done by code. Images can't be masked, so don't upload images that show personal data.
  • Never published. A draft containing a confidential term or an ID/card number gets a blocker, so it can't be submitted until it's fixed or a reviewer accepts a written reason.
  • Storage. {{ 'PostgreSQL' if storage == 'postgres' else 'SQLite' }}. Images are re-encoded (which strips camera metadata) before they are stored.
  • Demo data. Paisaleaf, its rules, facts and sample drafts are fictional. The demo is shared by everyone, so don't upload confidential material to it.
