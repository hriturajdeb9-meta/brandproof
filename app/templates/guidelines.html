What happens

  1. Read. Text is taken page by page. Colour codes are picked up by code for your palette.
  2. Draft. The AI turns each requirement into one small rule with a dimension, severity, do/don't examples, its page and the exact sentence it came from.
  3. Verify by code. Brandproof checks the quoted sentence is really on that page (and fixes the page if it's elsewhere). Banned words become code rules that are tested with their examples. Near-duplicates of your rules are flagged.
  4. You approve. Approve, edit or discard each one. Approved rules get an ID, a source and version 1, and apply to the next check.

The guideline is sent to the AI provider shown at the top. If the AI is busy, simple sentence patterns still find rules for you to finish by hand.
